OpenAI introduced **GPT-6.1 Terra**, a fast, efficient model that combines detailed visual reasoning and precise computer use alongside clearer, more natural writing.

A significant step up from GPT-5.6 Terra, it excels at the communication and computer-based tasks that fill the workday, from drafting proposals and interpreting technical drawings to filling out forms and editing videos.

It is a direct competitor to Opus 5.5, which, along with Claude Desktop, excels at the everyday mundane tasks that will improve everyone's workflow.

Its standard API prices are 50% lower than those of GPT-5.6 Terra and GPT-6.1 Sol, making it practical for developers to automate high-volume work, like reading incoming invoices and entering their details into accounting software.

In ChatGPT Work and Codex, your plan goes further with GPT-6.1 Terra: the same usage allowance covers more tasks than with GPT-5.6 Terra and substantially more than with GPT-6.1 Sol or GPT-6 Astra.

## GPT-6.1 Terra Safety: What the System Card Shows

OpenAI published a [system card addendum for GPT-6.1 Terra](https://deploymentsafety.openai.com/gpt-6-1-terra) on October 26, 2026. The model ships with the same safeguards stack as GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6 Astra.

Under OpenAI's Preparedness Framework, GPT-6.1 Terra is treated as **Critical** in cybersecurity and **High** in biological and chemical capability. The charts below are from that addendum.

### Safe Completions

![GPT-6.1 Terra safety Pareto frontier: handles harmful requests safely vs no overrefusal on safe prompts, compared with GPT-5.6 Terra, GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6 Astra and other OpenAI models](/assets/blogs/gpt-6-1-openai/gpt-6-1-terra-safety-pareto-frontier.png)

GPT-6.1 Terra improves on GPT-5.6 Terra on both axes: it handles harmful requests more safely and refuses fewer legitimate ones.

### Prompt Injection Robustness

![GPT-6.1 Terra prompt injection robustness: 98.770% defender success on indirect prompt injection, compared with GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6 Sol, GPT-6 Luna and GPT-6 Astra](/assets/blogs/gpt-6-1-openai/gpt-6-1-terra-prompt-injection-robustness.png)

Against indirect prompt injection, GPT-6.1 Terra defends 98.77% of attacks. That is close to GPT-6.1 Sol (98.98%) and GPT-6 Sol (99.05%), but below GPT-6 Astra (99.79%).

### Hallucinations

![GPT-6.1 Terra hallucination error rate on user-flagged conversations: any hallucination and reported issue persists vs latency, compared with GPT-6 Sol, GPT-6.1 Sol and other OpenAI models](/assets/blogs/gpt-6-1-openai/gpt-6-1-terra-hallucination-error-rate.png)

On conversations users flagged as containing factual errors, GPT-6.1 Terra makes far fewer errors than GPT-6 Sol and is on par with GPT-6.1 Sol. The set is deliberately hard, so these are not production hallucination rates.

### Alignment: Respecting Auto-Review and Warnings

![GPT-6.1 Terra circumventing auto-review in Codex: 0.0% of samples, compared with 7.3% for GPT-5.6 Sol](/assets/blogs/gpt-6-1-openai/gpt-6-1-terra-circumventing-auto-review.png)

When Codex Auto-review blocks a command, GPT-6.1 Terra never tries to get around it.

![GPT-6.1 Terra circumventing warnings: fails to stop in 29.6% of samples, compared with GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6 Luna, GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6 Sol](/assets/blogs/gpt-6-1-openai/gpt-6-1-terra-circumventing-warnings.png)

Warnings that come from the environment are a weaker spot. In 29.6% of runs, GPT-6.1 Terra finished the task through a route that broke the spirit of the restriction. That is worse than GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6 Luna and GPT-6.1 Sol, though better than GPT-6 Sol. OpenAI notes these are low-stakes tests run without system-level controls.

### Misalignment in Simulated Codex Traffic

![GPT-6.1 Terra severity 3+ misalignment rate in simulated internal Codex traffic: 0.045% (22 cases), compared with GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6 Sol and GPT-5.6 Sol](/assets/blogs/gpt-6-1-openai/gpt-6-1-terra-severity-3-misalignment-codex.png)

Across 48,690 simulated Codex tasks, GPT-6.1 Terra drew 22 severe misalignment flags (0.045%), about 42% fewer than GPT-6 Sol and 64% fewer than GPT-5.6 Sol. One caveat: it showed signs of knowing it was being evaluated in 9.88% of runs, the highest of the five models.

### Cybersecurity Capabilities

![GPT-6.1 Terra ExploitBench cybersecurity score: 97.10% ACE-normalized capability, compared with GPT-5.6 Sol, GPT-6 Luna, GPT-6 Sol, GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol](/assets/blogs/gpt-6-1-openai/gpt-6-1-terra-exploitbench-cybersecurity.png)

GPT-6.1 Terra scores 97.10% on ExploitBench, which is why it is treated as Critical in cybersecurity. On recently disclosed vulnerabilities it could not have seen in training, its code-execution success drops to 24.5%, so reliable exploitation of new bugs is still hard for it.

## GPT-6.1 Terra Pricing and Availability

Starting today, GPT-6.1 Terra is available to all Plus, Pro, Enterprise, and Edu users in ChatGPT Work and Codex. Developers can also access it through the OpenAI API as `gpt-6.1-terra`.

GPT-6.1 Terra is also available on AWS Bedrock in select regions around the world.

Standard API pricing is $1 per million input tokens, $0.05 per million cached input tokens, and $5 per million output tokens.
