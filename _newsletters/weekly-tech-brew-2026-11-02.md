### 1. Binance Crypto Hack

World's largest cryptocurrency exchange **Binance** was hit by hackers today, who stole **$6.3 billion** from the platform in the biggest crypto heist in history.

#### What Happened?

Around $6.3 billion worth of cryptocurrencies were stolen from wallets that Binance uses to process transactions, the exchange said. In a statement, Binance said it first believed the attack happened due to a compromised laptop.

"However, our subsequent investigation has uncovered additional points of breach," it added.

#### How Did Hackers Steal The Money?

The global centralised exchange of Binance, Binance.com, was compromised during the hack. Binance.us, which is meant for US users, was not affected, it said.

The exchange further said that the compromised laptop "gave an attacker access to a service container, where they submitted unauthorised withdrawal transactions."

Richard Teng, Binance's co-chief executive officer, described it as a "complex attack process".

According to US blockchain analytics firm TRM Labs, the attackers compromised Binance's "signing infrastructure", which authorizes transactions, allowing them to issue instructions that emptied the affected wallets.

The bulk of the heist unfolded in about 20 minutes, blockchain records show, beginning shortly after 2000 GMT on Thursday.

Once the funds were moved, the attackers raced to shift them beyond the reach of Binance counterparties and stablecoin issuers, which have frozen roughly half of the stolen assets.

#### Who Did It?

No confirmation from Binance or any other authority has come regarding who is behind this hacking. However, a US-based blockchain intelligence firm says a North Korean unit was likely behind the hack.

TRM, in a post on its website on Thursday, attributed the attack to TraderTraitor, which it said was one of North Korea's elite hacking unit. "We make this judgment with high confidence," the firm said.

Blockchain analysis company Chainalysis also believed the Binance hack was "likely done by DPRK (North Korea) group TraderTraitor," its co-founder and CEO Jonathan Levin wrote on X.

#### What Will Happen To Binance Users?

Binance said on Friday it has reserved more than $6.34 billion in company funds, including $5 billion already held in impacted token assets, to deal with the fallout of the attack.

In an earlier statement, Teng said Binance users' funds are safe. The platform also suspended all withdrawals and deposits temporarily as part of a safety measure.

Binance said it was working with its partners to freeze assets worth $3 billion, which have been located so far.

That doesn't mean the coins will be returned to Binance, but it significantly limits the attackers' ability to convert them into cash.

"We are still on track towards the expected timeline to resume the withdrawal service in 45 hours," Binance said.

#### Bitcoin Slides Below $77,000 as Record $6.3B Binance Hack Rattles Crypto

*Bitcoin and other tokens were already under pressure this week with broader macro forces weighing on the market.*

Bitcoin sank below $77,000 for the first time in more than a month, deepening a selloff after Binance Holdings revealed a record hack that rattled the crypto industry and renewed questions about the security of customer assets — even at the world's largest digital-asset exchange.

The original cryptocurrency declined as much as 3.1% on Friday to fall as low as $76,300, though it slightly recovered in mid-afternoon trading in New York. Other tokens, including some that were impacted by the security breach, also fell. Binance's BNB token has declined about 7% since Wednesday to around $700.

### 2. Israel Election Results 2026

Israel election officials reject Netanyahu's **"stolen vote"** claim, say results stand.

<details class="nl-expand nl-expand--release">
<summary><span class="nl-expand-more">Expand</span><span class="nl-expand-less" hidden>Collapse</span></summary>
<div class="nl-expand-body" markdown="1">

![Israel election results 2026: Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu waves from a Likud party podium after the vote](/assets/newsletter/weekly-tech-brew-2026-11-02/israel-election-results-2026-netanyahu-likud.png){: .nl-hero}

#### Final Results: Netanyahu's Bloc 53, Eisenkot's 48, Arab Parties 19

The Central Elections Committee (CEC) published the final results of the 2026 national election in the early hours of Friday. Turnout was **77.9%**, the highest in decades, with **5,716,665** votes cast.

**Seats by party:** Likud 27, Yashar 21, B'Yachad 12, the Joint List 11, The Democrats 8, Ra'am 8, Yisrael Beytenu 7, United Torah Judaism 7, Otzma Yehudit 7, Shas 6 and Religious Zionism 6.

**Below the 3.25% threshold:** Yoaz Hendel's Reservists-Economic party (2.63%) and Ofer Winter's People of Israel (1.7%).

**A big drop for Netanyahu.** The pro-Netanyahu bloc, which forms the current government, falls to **53 seats** from 68 in the outgoing Knesset. The anti-Netanyahu bloc has **48**.

**A record for Arab parties.** The Joint List and Ra'am won an all-time high of **19 seats**, almost double their current 10. That would be enough to give the anti-Netanyahu bloc a comfortable majority, but every Zionist party in that bloc except The Democrats has ruled out working with Ra'am or the Joint List.

#### Officials: Fraud Claims "Entirely Unfounded"

Amid reports of a possible Qatari campaign to influence the vote, the CEC rejected claims of widespread voter fraud.

"Reports published throughout the day are entirely unfounded," said acting director Dean Livne. The investigation "will not affect the final results submitted to the president, nor does it concern any particular sector of the population."

#### What Happens Next?

- The official results go to **President Isaac Herzog on Wednesday**. He will then meet each party leader to hear who they recommend to form the next government.
- The Knesset will be sworn in on **November 10**. Coalition talks could drag on for more than two months, with neither side close to the **61-seat majority** needed.
- Herzog urged leaders to avoid another round of elections: "I expect everyone who comes here to come with an open mind."
- Both **Netanyahu** and **Eisenkot** have vowed that they will be the one to form the next government.

The vote came four years after a three-year stretch in which Israel went to the polls five times, the first four failing to produce a stable government.

### 6. oruk-loop-153M: A Looped Speech Recognition Model With 13× Fewer Parameters

**Oruk** replaced 52 speech-model layers with two reusable blocks, and is releasing **oruk-loop-153M** with open weights.

The encoder runs one shared block **24 times**. The decoder runs another **28 times**. Each pass builds on the last while reusing the same weights. Small adapters customize each pass.

That gives **153 million parameters** compared with about **2.04 billion** in the Qwen3-ASR teacher: roughly **13× fewer parameters** to store. Looping changes how much of the model has to be stored while keeping the repeated computation.

**The win is storage.** Compute per token stays close to the teacher's, and default merged decoding uses about as much GPU memory as the teacher.

The weights ship alongside inference code and a model card with the training recipe and evaluation limits. The benchmark results use checkpoints selected on those same test sets, and performance varies across domains.

### 8. NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe: Open-Weight Speech Recognition in 40 Languages

**NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe** is an open-weight, offline speech and audio model for transcription in **40 languages**, with support for recordings up to **one hour** and a **65,536-token context**.

Its hybrid **Mamba-2–Transformer mixture-of-experts** architecture has 34B total parameters but activates only about **4.3B**, including the audio encoder. It is released in BF16, FP8 and NVFP4 precision and runs on a single H100 80 GB.

**One model, many ways to write down what was said.** You pick the transcription style with the instruction: verbatim, clean written text, domain vocabulary, speaker-attributed transcripts with word timestamps, speech translation, language identification, or questions about the audio.

**How it scores:**

- **4.71% mean WER** on the English Open ASR Leaderboard, the best open-weight result (next best: Qwen3-ASR-1.7B at 4.94)
- **10.98% WER** on long-form audio (next best open-weight: Parakeet-TDT-0.6B-v3 at 13.37)
- **4.62% WER** on the Multilingual Open ASR Leaderboard (next best open-weight: Qwen3-ASR-1.7B at 6.49)
- **15.80 mean cpWER** on multi-speaker meetings and calls, the best published result

**It's fast, too.** With the optimized Nemotron Transcribe NIM, one RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell GPU transcribes up to **2,238 seconds of audio per second** and returns a 30-second clip's transcript in **0.13 seconds**. Those serving optimizations are not yet in the open-source release; NVIDIA says the release-day vLLM integration is roughly two times slower.

It is an **offline** model, not a real-time streaming ASR service, and it is released under the **OpenMDW-1.1** license for commercial and non-commercial use.

### Harry Kane Wins Ballon d'Or 2026

![Harry Kane wins the Ballon d'Or 2026: the Bayern Munich and England striker smiles as he holds up the golden ball trophy on stage](/assets/newsletter/weekly-tech-brew-2026-11-02/harry-kane-ballon-dor-2026.png){: .nl-hero}

**Harry Kane** edged out **Kylian Mbappé** for the 2026 Men's Ballon d'Or by just **28 points**, making it one of the closest votes in the award's history.

<details class="nl-expand nl-expand--release">
<summary><span class="nl-expand-more">Expand</span><span class="nl-expand-less" hidden>Collapse</span></summary>
<div class="nl-expand-body" markdown="1">

#### A First for England in 25 Years

Bayern Munich striker Kane became the **first English winner of the prize in 25 years** on Monday, as Bayern celebrated their first Ballon d'Or winner in 45 years, and the first for any player at a German club since 1996.

The England international scored **73 goals** for club and country last season, when he was Europe's top scorer, and his six goals at the World Cup helped his country to a third-place finish.

Mbappé, meanwhile, struck 42 goals for Real Madrid last season before becoming the **top scorer in World Cup history** this summer with 22 goals for France, 10 of them scored at this year's edition.

#### How Close Was the Vote?

The full voting breakdown, published by French outlet L'Equipe on Friday ahead of award organizers France Football's print version on Saturday, showed just how close the race was between the two strikers.

Kane garnered **40 first-place votes** for a total of **1,055 points**, compared to 23 first-place votes for runner-up Mbappé and a total of **1,027 points**. That made it the **fourth-closest vote** in the award's 70-year history, according to L'Equipe, and the closest since Lionel Messi beat Virgil van Dijk by seven points in 2019.

The prize is decided by a jury of 100 international journalists. The points system awards 15 points for first place, 12 for second, 10 for third, then 8, 7, 5, 4, 3, 2, and 1 point for the rest of the top 10.

Third-place **Lamine Yamal** was a further 146 points back of Mbappé, and took 13 first-place votes, after a standout year that saw the 19-year-old help Barcelona win the LaLiga title before playing a key part in Spain's World Cup triumph.

There was a split in the votes for the World Cup champions, with Yamal's teammate, **Rodri**, in fourth with 726 points and 10 first-place votes.

#### Women's Ballon d'Or: A Third Win for Alexia Putellas

There was a more decisive outcome for the Women's Ballon d'Or award. **Alexia Putellas** (618 points) won the award for a **third time** by beating her former Barcelona teammate Ewa Pajor (532 points) by 86 points. Putellas, now playing for London City Lionesses, was selected first by 28 of the 50 judges.

Manchester City's Khadija Shaw was a distant third with 344 points.
